Accessibility & mobility · Cyberabad Moves

Our panels at Cyberabad Moves

Cyberabad Moves is a three-day mobility exhibition at T-Hub, Raidurgam, from 26 to 28 November 2026, 9 am to 6 pm. We made two panels for it: how far people walk to a bus, Metro or MMTS stop, and how comfortable that walk is.

Organised by the Cyberabad Municipal Corporation, Cyberabad Police and the Society for Cyberabad Security Council, in partnership with the SHIFT Transport coalition. More at cyberabadmoves.in.

Panel 1

Mapping access to transit

Walking time to the nearest bus, Metro and MMTS stop across the city, and the three corporations (CMC, GHMC, MMC) compared on stops and everyday places. 59% of the built-up area is within a 10-minute walk of a stop.

How we collect the data

The numbers on the panels come from our Accessibility Atlas. It works out how far you can walk along the streets, at 4.5 km/h adjusted for slope, and counts the stops and everyday places within reach. Heat comes from the weather stations of the Telangana Development Planning Society. Sources and method are on the Atlas.

Maps show shade, slope and heat, but not everything that makes a walk comfortable: footpaths, safe crossings and proper shelters at bus stops. For those we ask the people who use them, through short surveys. Everything they send is published as open data.
