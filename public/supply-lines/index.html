Water · HMWSSB area

Which river reaches which part of Hyderabad

When HMWSSB shuts one of its supply lines for repairs, the news reports which areas will lose water. We read 78 such notices, from April 2022 to November 2026, and mapped the places they name by the line that was shut.

How we made this

  • We collected 78 HMWSSB shutdown notices from the news. 54 of them name the line being worked on, and only these are used. One exception: the notice for the Asif Nagar filter beds in May 2026 counts as Osman Sagar, because 20 of its 21 areas are those of the Osman Sagar conduit notice of November 2026.
  • The area names were cleaned and their spellings joined, so Autonagar and Auto Nagar are one place. Where one name is used for two places, the areas listed beside it in each notice decide which one is meant, as with Bharat Nagar and Gautam Nagar.
  • A place is shown on a line when at least two notices cut its supply on that line. For the twin lakes, which are rarely shut, one notice is enough. Areas listed only at low pressure are not counted.
  • A place named on two lines takes the one named more often. Four places are named equally often on two lines.
  • The places were geocoded, so the points are approximate.
  • A notice shows where a line reached on the day of the work. HMWSSB moves water between lines, so a place may get water from more than one.

Sources

The 54 notices behind the map, in order of the work date. The numbers are those shown for each place.
